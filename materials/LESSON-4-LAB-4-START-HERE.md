# Lesson 4 + Lab 4

This project contains the materials for the final Math Camp coding lesson and lab.

## Lesson 4: Tuesday, August 25, 3:00–5:00 p.m.

1. Open `math-camp-2026.Rproj` in RStudio.
2. Place `slides/lesson-4/lesson-4.pdf` beside RStudio.
3. Open `code/lesson-4-walkthrough.R` and run one numbered section at a time.
4. During the Quarto demonstration, open `materials/lesson-4-demo.qmd` and click **Render**.

The lesson reviews the functions used across Math Camp and shows how a policy question, table, figure, finding, and limitation fit in one reproducible report.

## Lab 4: Wednesday, August 26, 4:00–5:00 p.m.

Read `labs/lab-4/handout/lab-4-handout.pdf`. Choose one starter under `materials/lab-4/`:

- `health-brief.qmd`
- `gender-brief.qmd`
- `access-brief.qmd`
- `climate-brief.qmd`

Each starter imports its prepared CSV from `data/lesson-4/`. Complete one table, one figure, one finding, one limitation, and one AI-verification note. Save the first interpretation before asking Codex to work read-only. Click **Render** in RStudio to create the standalone HTML brief.

No model is required. No graded submission is required.
