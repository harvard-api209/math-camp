THE INVESTIGATION

Four questions, one workflow.

Lesson 1 assigned work among the analyst, R, and an agent. Lesson 2 constructed a documented analysis table. Lesson 3 connected observations, plots, and descriptive claims. Lesson 4 carries those decisions into a rendered policy brief.

The four tracks cover health, gender, infrastructure access, and climate transitions. Health and gender are 2022 cross-sections. Access and climate use common endpoints so every economy is compared over the same period.

RENDERED REPORT

Prose, code, and output remain together.

R Markdown and Quarto both combine narrative with executable code. New Math Camp briefs use Quarto because the same framework supports reports, slides, and the student book. Rendering starts a fresh R session and executes chunks in order, exposing missing objects or paths that Console history can hide.

COMPLETE BRIEF

State the question, source, unit, sample, and period. Produce one table and one figure from the same comparison. Write one supported finding, one limitation, and one AI verification note.

Codex enters after the first interpretation has been saved. It works read-only, reproduces the table, inspects the figure mappings, and challenges one consequential statement. The analyst independently verifies one material statement before accepting, revising, or rejecting it.
