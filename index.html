Code, data, and judgment.

A practical introduction to R, Quarto, and coding agents for international development policy.

Math Camp begins with questions, not commands. You will examine development data, make a claim precise, ask an agent for help, and decide what evidence is strong enough to trust.

Everyone receives a working dataset and code scaffold. Students new to programming can follow the main path. Experienced programmers can update the data, test alternate specifications, and challenge the agent.

POLICY QUESTIONS

One file. Four ways into development.

Each working group follows one question during the labs. These are associational questions. A pattern is not automatically a policy effect.

  1. HEALTHHow is national income associated with under-five mortality, and does the pattern differ by income group?GDP per capita, under-five mortality, income classification
  2. GENDERWhere is girls' secondary enrollment associated with lower adolescent fertility, and where does the relationship depart from the overall pattern?Female secondary enrollment, adolescent fertility
  3. ACCESSDoes electricity access travel with internet access, and which countries closed the combined access gap fastest?Electricity access, internet use, region
  4. CLIMATECan renewable electricity expand while carbon intensity falls and income continues to grow?Renewable electricity, carbon intensity, GDP per capita growth
