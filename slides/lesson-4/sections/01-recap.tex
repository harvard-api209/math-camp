\begin{frame}{Four questions remain available}
\small
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Health.} How is income associated with under-five mortality in 2022, and how does the pattern differ by income group?
\item \textbf{Gender.} Where is girls' secondary enrollment associated with lower adolescent fertility, and where does the pattern depart from the overall comparison?
\item \textbf{Access.} Which economies closed the combined electricity and internet access gap fastest from 2000 to 2022?
\item \textbf{Climate.} Which economies expanded renewable electricity, reduced carbon intensity, and ended with higher real income from 2000 to 2021?
\end{enumerate}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{The final student artifact}
One rendered Quarto brief contains:
\vspace{0.25cm}
\begin{enumerate}
\item the policy question;
\item source, unit, sample, and period;
\item one table;
\item one figure;
\item one supported finding;
\item one limitation; and
\item one AI-use and verification note.
\end{enumerate}
\vspace{0.25cm}
The artifact is optional and ungraded. Its value is that every result remains connected to code and evidence.
\end{frame}
