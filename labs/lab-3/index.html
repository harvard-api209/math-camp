THE BRIEF

Make one comparison legible.

Continue with your selected policy track. Work beside others and borrow ideas, but keep your own code. The goal is not the perfect model. It is an inspectable chain from question to evidence to claim.

DONE WHEN

Your figure has units and a readable caption, your model can be interpreted in plain language, and your claim includes a limitation.

35 MINUTES

Build a small evidence bundle.

00-08Question

Rewrite it as descriptive or associational.

08-18Figure

Choose encodings, labels, and a defensible comparison.

18-28Model

Fit one simple specification and interpret a coefficient.

28-35Diagnose

Inspect missing rows, residuals, and one surprise.

  • □The figure and model use the same analysis sample, or I explain why not.
  • □The axes and interpretation name the units.
  • □I checked whether a transformation changes the visual story.
  • □I can identify one plausible omitted factor.

15 MINUTES

Ask the agent to try to break the claim.

CRITIC PROMPT
Act as a skeptical reviewer. Do not edit files. First check the R code and analysis sample. Then check the statistical interpretation. Identify the strongest sentence supported by the evidence and any sentence that goes too far. Cite the exact output behind each judgment.

Accept no criticism you cannot trace. For each item, mark it verified, rejected, or unresolved and record the check you used.

LAST 10 MINUTES

Tell the room what changed your mind.

Share the original claim, the revised claim, and the check that forced the revision. This is a conversation, not a presentation.
