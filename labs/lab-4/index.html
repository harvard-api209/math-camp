THE BRIEF

Can another person make the evidence move?

Bring whatever you have: an R script, Quarto notebook, figure, or even a documented partial attempt. Pair with someone on another policy track. Nothing is collected, ranked, or graded.

THE SPIRIT

A useful failure is a success. The purpose is to expose hidden setup, unclear definitions, fragile paths, and claims that need more support before the fall semester begins.

20 MINUTES

Run the handoff cold.

00-03Exchange

Share the project without a verbal tour.

03-10Orient

Read the question, README, and data note.

10-17Run

Start a clean session and reproduce the output.

17-20Record

Note the first blocker and the first strong choice.

25 MINUTES

Human review, agent review, repair.

  • □Reproducibility: can the artifact be rebuilt from the source?
  • □Provenance: are indicators, years, units, and retrieval date visible?
  • □Interpretation: does the claim fit the design and output?
  • □Communication: can a new reader understand the figure?
  • □AI use: are agent contributions and human checks described?

After the human pass, ask an agent to audit the same project. Compare disagreements before repairing one high-value issue.

LAST 15 MINUTES

Ninety seconds, three sentences.

SHOW AND TELL
Our question was...
The most important pattern or failure was...
The next check we would run is...

Your optional field notebook stays yours. The camp ends with a workflow you can reuse, not a grade.
